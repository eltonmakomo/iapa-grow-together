# IAPA Grow Together

Redesign the International Agribusiness Practitioners Association (IAPA) website as a modern, mobile-first one-pager in English and French. Use the uploaded website architecture to guide the page, the 2026–2030 Strategic Plan for mission and priorities, and the presentation, flyer, and newsletters for services, evidence, and stories.

Open with authentic agribusiness photography, a clear headline about building resilient and inclusive agricultural value chains, and two calls to action: “Join IAPA” and “Partner with Us.” Use a sticky header with anchor navigation and an EN/FR switch that keeps visitors in the same section.

Structure the page around Who We Are, Our Work, Training, Knowledge Hub, and Join Us. Introduce IAPA’s pan-African network and three strategic pillars. Showcase advisory services, certification, the four Business Schools, and Inclusive Contract Farming. Feature downloadable cards for IAPA Pulse Issue 3 (October 2026) and Issue 2 (June 2026). Close with clear membership and partnership enquiry options and verified contact details.

Use the supplied logos and Open Sans. Pair deep green (#3A7C1E) with warm off-white and sparing yellow, orange, and savanna green accents. Use bold typography, generous spacing, and subtle curves inspired by the logo. Keep the page fast and accessible, with strong contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation.

Keep copy concise. Clearly distinguish IAPA’s own results, shared ABF-GIZ/IAPA results, and future targets. Use verified figures, partner names, and links from the uploaded materials.
